About the project

We built “Customer segments” from scratch as part of Ozon Seller CRM. The brief came from the SPO: give sellers a tool to work with their customer base

Sellers see each group’s metrics and recommended actions

The section groups customers by their purchases and interest in products

The MVP included ready-made segments and sub-segments. Creating custom segments was left out of the first launch

Problems

01

Overview and details

The overview needs a view of the whole base, and each segment needs details for working with that specific audience

02

From numbers to action

Analytics need recommendations next to them: what goal to pursue with a group and which offer to use

03

Different sellers

Some already have repeat customers; others only have first orders or interest in their products

Concepts

We started by taking stock of the customer data: RFM segments, cart, favourites, views, average order value, demographics, regions and categories. For each one we decided what the seller needs here and what they already look at in other parts of the account.

Understanding the audience

The interface’s main job became linking customer data to the next action. The overview shows repeat, new and potential customers, their metrics and recommendations for each group.

I proposed highlighting a key segment and expanding on it in the overview. That gives sellers a starting point: they see the audience worth focusing on, with possible actions right next to its metrics.

Pro: sellers have a starting point. Con: the other segments get less attention.

Why: most sellers don’t know where to start.

Recommendation setup

In CRM you can start from a ready-made scenario for a goal: keep repeat customers, motivate new ones to buy again or grow the audience. For a custom setup, you can start from scratch.

To bring the different mechanics into a clear process, I split it into three steps:

01

Who to send to

Choose the audience

02

What to offer

Use a promotion, bonuses or tell them about products

03

How to show it

Set up the right format and landing page

1 of 4

Explain the conditions before sending

Behind the shared flow are different constraints. A regular message can’t be sent to every audience; campaigns have subscription conditions and a recipient limit; the send date depends on how long the promotion runs.

Support working with an individual customer

The customer list complements mass communication. It brings together purchases, recent actions, interests and recommended products. Sellers can filter the audience and switch to one-to-one conversation when chat is available.

For deeper analysis of a group, the existing customer portrait with segment support is built into CRM. And a widget on the account home page shows recent customer interest and suggests an action, so working with the audience can start even before opening the section.

Keep going after sending

In the campaign list, sellers see the status, number of recipients, opens and clicks. Recommendations help them decide whether to repeat a successful message or rethink the offer.

Results

The result is a coherent CRM flow: understand the audience, choose an action, set up the communication and assess the response. My main contribution: the key segment concept and the step-by-step setup, which give working with the product a clear sequence.
